## Avoiding Tick Bites

The best way to prevent Lyme disease is to avoid tick bites. CDC recommends using EPA-registered insect repellents (such as those containing DEET, picaridin, IR3535, oil of lemon eucalyptus, para-menthane-diol, or 2-undecanone), wearing long sleeves and pants or clothing pre-treated with 0.5% permethrin, and checking your body for ticks after spending time outdoors.

## Removing Ticks Promptly

Ticks should be removed as soon as possible, since delaying removal increases the risk of infection.
