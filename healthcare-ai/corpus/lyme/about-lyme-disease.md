## What Is Lyme Disease

Lyme disease is a bacterial infection spread to people through the bite of infected blacklegged ticks. In the United States, it is caused by the bacterium Borrelia burgdorferi and, rarely, Borrelia mayonii.

## How Common It Is

It is the most common vector-borne illness reported in the United States and is most frequently reported in the Northeast, mid-Atlantic, and upper-Midwest regions, where infected ticks are most common in forested and grassy areas.
