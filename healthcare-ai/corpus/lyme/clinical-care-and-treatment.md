## Standard Treatment

Lyme disease is generally treated with a course of oral antibiotics. Early diagnosis and proper antibiotic treatment are important and can help prevent more serious symptoms from developing.

## Commonly Used Antibiotics

Commonly used antibiotics include doxycycline, amoxicillin, and cefuroxime; the specific choice and duration depend on the patient's age, symptoms, and stage of disease. Most people treated in the early stages recover quickly and completely.
