## Early Symptoms

Typical early signs and symptoms of Lyme disease include fever, chills, headache, fatigue, muscle and joint aches, swollen lymph nodes, and an expanding skin rash called erythema migrans, which can look like a "bull's-eye."

## Symptoms If Left Untreated

If left untreated, the infection can spread from the site of the bite to other parts of the body, producing a wider range of symptoms including facial palsy, arthritis (especially in the knees), heart rhythm irregularities, and nervous system problems.
