## Tick Transmission

Lyme disease is spread to people through the bite of an infected blacklegged tick (also called a deer tick).

## Attachment Time and Risk

In most cases, a tick needs to be attached for more than 24 hours before it can transmit the Lyme disease bacteria, so finding and removing an attached tick promptly can reduce the risk of infection.
