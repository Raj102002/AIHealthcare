# Demo Video Script — ClearSignal

Target length: **4 minutes** (fits the assignment's 3–5 min window with room to breathe).
Record as a screen capture of [healwithaura.netlify.app](https://healwithaura.netlify.app/chat) with
voiceover narration; captions can be auto-generated by YouTube/CapCut/Descript from the audio track and
then lightly corrected (medical terms and route names are the usual auto-caption failure points — check
"Lyme", "serology", "ClearSignal", "handoff" specifically).

Recording checklist before you hit record:
- [ ] Sign in with a demo account that already has a few days of journal entries seeded (empty state is
      not compelling on camera — use the "Ask Your Journal" example in Scene 4 as your cue for what data
      to seed).
- [ ] Close other browser tabs/notifications; use a clean browser profile.
- [ ] Confirm `/admin` has some real request history to show (hit a few routes beforehand if it's empty).
- [ ] Do one full silent dry-run first to check timing before recording narration for real.

---

## Scene 1 — Problem (0:00–0:45)

**Visual:** Title card or slide 2 of the pitch deck (`artifacts/ClearSignal_Pitch_Deck.pptx`), or simply
the ClearSignal landing page.

**Narration:**
> "Standard Lyme disease testing detects your antibody response, not the bacteria itself — and that
> response takes three to six weeks to develop. So a patient with a real, active infection most likely
> tests negative early on. That negative result then anchors every later clinician away from the right
> diagnosis. This is a timing problem, not an information problem — and it falls disproportionately on
> women, whose symptoms get read as stress once a negative test is in the chart. I'm Raja, and this is
> ClearSignal, built for the LymeX Innovation Accelerator through HHS OASH and the NIH Office of Research
> on Women's Health."

## Scene 2 — Chat + RAG grounding (0:45–1:45)

**Visual:** Navigate to `/chat`. Type a real lay-phrased question (e.g. "I had a bullseye rash three
weeks ago and now I'm exhausted all the time, is this Lyme?"). Show the response streaming in, then
scroll to show the cited source.

**Narration:**
> "The core is a hybrid RAG chat — dense vector search plus keyword search, fused and reranked, grounded
> in real CDC Lyme disease data. Watch it cite its source here. If a question falls outside what the
> corpus actually supports, it says so instead of guessing — that threshold is hard-coded, not a
> suggestion to the model."

Optional: demonstrate voice input (mic button) for ~10 seconds if the mic reliably works in your
recording environment — skip this beat entirely rather than risk a live failure on camera.

## Scene 3 — Negative-test contextualizer (1:45–2:15)

**Visual:** Navigate to `/test-context`. Show entering a negative test result and days-since-exposure,
and the resulting explanation.

**Narration:**
> "This page exists because of that timing gap directly — it takes a negative test result and
> contextualizes it against real CDC sensitivity data, so a negative-at-two-weeks doesn't get treated as
> a final answer."

## Scene 4 — Journal + agentic AI (2:15–3:15)

**Visual:** Navigate to `/journal`. Show the Symptoms/Function log briefly, then switch to the "Ask Your
Journal" tab and ask a real question (e.g. "how has my fatigue trended over the last two weeks?").
Show the tool-calling response.

**Narration:**
> "The journal captures good days as well as bad ones — most tracking tools only log flare-ups, which
> makes episodic illness look continuous. And this tab is genuinely agentic, not scripted: it's a Groq
> tool-calling loop over six real tools against your own logged data. Ask it about your trend, and it
> actually computes the slope — it's not a canned response."

## Scene 5 — Handoff document + safety layer (3:15–3:45)

**Visual:** Navigate to `/handoff`, generate a document, show the one-page output. Briefly mention (can
be voiceover-only, no need to trigger it live) the red-flag layer.

**Narration:**
> "All of this compiles into a one-page, clinician-ready handoff document. And underneath every feature
> is a deterministic red-flag layer — chest pain, stroke signs, anaphylaxis — that runs before any model
> call, with zero AI in that decision path. A hundred percent escalation recall on our gold set, zero
> false positives."

## Scene 6 — Close (3:45–4:00)

**Visual:** `/admin` dashboard for 3–4 seconds (shows this is production-engineered, not just a demo),
then back to a title/contact card.

**Narration:**
> "ClearSignal is live at healwithaura.netlify.app, fully documented, containerized, and eval-tested.
> Thanks for watching — happy to answer questions."
